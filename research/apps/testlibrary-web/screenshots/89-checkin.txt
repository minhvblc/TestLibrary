💡 Why your brain loves unfinished tasks

THE SCIENCE

The Zeigarnik effect describes how incomplete tasks stay 'active' in memory, nagging at you until they're closed. For ADHD brains, which already struggle to filter open loops, this can feel like a dozen browser tabs running at once.

TRY THIS

Use it on purpose: start a dreaded task for two minutes and stop mid-way. Your brain will pull you back to finish it.
